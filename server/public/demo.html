Is there any public art on Montreal's REM?
AI

Yes! The REM has a dedicated public art program.

The REM's permanent artworks are valued at $7.3 million, and temporary works are being created in collaboration with four Montreal universities.[1] A separate $5 million budget funds the temporary artworks created by university students.[1]

In fall 2024, two permanent artworks were inaugurated at the Brossard and Panama stations.[2] In February 2025, CDPQ Infra revealed the names of the artists creating public artworks for five new REM stations.[3]
